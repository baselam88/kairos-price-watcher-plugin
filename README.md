# Competitor Price Watcher

Produces a weekly digest of competitors' public prices and promotions from public sources only.

A free plugin from KAIROS AI Systems — one focused tool that does one job well for a
small business: no suite to learn, no setup project, no account required beyond your
agent host.

## What it does

Fetch named competitors' public pages on schedule, diff against last week, output the change digest. Public data only; weeks of early visibility is the metric.

## How to use it

Add this plugin to a compatible agent host (Cursor, Grok Bot, or another
agent-plugins host), then ask in plain language, for example:

> Use the Competitor Price Watcher to help me: produces a weekly digest of competitors' public prices and promotions from public sources only — I'll paste the details.

The plugin works from the facts you give it — your services, your prices, your
records. It never invents a number, a policy, or a result; anything it projects is
labelled an estimate with its basis. Drafts that would go to a customer stay drafts
until you approve them.

This plugin also connects to the Competitor Price Watcher service endpoint (`kairos-price-watcher-mcp`) for the live checks described in the skill; the guided workflow works the same in chat.
